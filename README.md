# Privacy Policy for SmartScan AI

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Application Name:** SmartScan: PDF & Photo Resizer  
**Developer:** SmartScan AI Team  

SmartScan AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to total user privacy and digital data protection. This Privacy Policy certifies that our application operates on a 100% On-Device, Offline-First architecture in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards and Google Play Developer Policies.

---

### 1. 100% On-Device Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)
SmartScan AI processes all documents, photos, scans, and PDFs locally inside your device's secure hardware sandbox:
* **Document Scanning & Multi-Page PDF Creation:** Auto-edge detection, crop, and PDF generation happen 100% offline.
* **ID Card Double-Side Scanner:** 2-sided identity card merging and digital Self-Attestation stamping operate entirely on-device.
* **Studio Passport Photo Maker:** Background removal, color adjustments, and 36-photo printable A4 sheets are generated locally.
* **Application Photo & Signature Resizer:** Exact KB compression (20KB - 50KB) and pixel dimension resizing happen locally without uploading images to any server.
* **Visiting Card Scanner:** Deep OCR text recognition extracts business card details directly to your local address book.
* **PDF Power Utilities:** Merging, splitting, and compressing PDF files happen offline on your phone storage.
* **QR & Barcode Suite:** QR code generation and live camera decoding work without sending data over the internet.

**Zero files, scans, photos, signatures, or documents are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any external server or cloud database.**

---

### 2. Information Collection & Permissions
SmartScan AI does NOT collect, sell, rent, or store any personal data or document content. The app requires only minimum necessary device permissions to function:
* **Camera Permission:** Used solely for capturing real-time documents, business cards, QR codes, and photos. No images are saved without explicit user action.
* **Storage / Photos Permission:** Used solely to read user-selected images for resizing/converting and to save generated PDFs and photos into the user's local storage.
