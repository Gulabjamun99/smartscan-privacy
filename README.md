# smartscan-privacy
Privacy Policy for SmartScan AI

Effective Date: September 2, 2026
Developer: SmartScan AI Developer

SmartScan AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to total user privacy and data protection. This Privacy Policy certifies that our application operates on a 100% On-Device, Offline-First architecture.

1. 100% On-Device Local Processing (Zero Cloud Uploads)
All features in SmartScan AI operate entirely within your device's local hardware sandbox:
• Document Scanning & Multi-Page PDF Creation
• ID Card Double-Side Scanner & Anti-Fraud Self-Attestation Stamping
• Studio Passport Photo Generator & A4 Print Grid Sheet Alignment
• Govt Exam Photo & Signature KB/Pixel Resizer (SSC, UPSC, NTA)
• Visiting Card Scanner & VCF Contact Export
• QR Code & Barcode Suite (Generation & Scanning)
• PDF Power Tools (Merge, Split, and Compression)
• Image Editor Studio & Filters

Zero files, photos, identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License), signatures, or scans are ever transmitted to or stored on any external servers or cloud databases.

2. Information Collection and Device Permissions
• Personal Identity Data: We do NOT collect, store, track, or share any personal identity data, document numbers, names, or contact lists.
• Camera Access: Used in real-time exclusively on your device to capture documents, ID cards, and photos that you explicitly choose to scan.
• Storage Access: Used solely to read selected gallery photos and save generated PDFs and image files directly to your device's local storage (Downloads/Pictures).

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising
• Google AdMob: Delivers compliant, non-intrusive advertisements in the free version in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.
• Google Play In-App Billing: Handles optional Ad-Free Pro upgrades securely through Google Play. Payment and financial details are handled directly by Google; the developer never accesses or stores your payment credentials.

4. Statutory Compliance & Legal Safety
SmartScan AI strictly complies with:
• India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
• Information Technology Act, 2000 & UIDAI document safety guidelines
• Global privacy frameworks including GDPR & CCPA.
